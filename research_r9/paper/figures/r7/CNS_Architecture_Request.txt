   Observable residual, inlier rate, step, weak pose information, prior scale

PROPOSED CAPTION
Architecture of executable operating contracts. Frozen forecasts and population
event cards connect a task declared before sensing to a compatible policy.
Causal scan processing feeds retained point-to-plane registration; forecasts,
profile predicates and time predicates govern publication and further work.
The receiver replaces the contribution of the same scan source and replays
measured motion, restoring the source prior after rejected completion. A
separate metadata service attaches applicable population event bounds only
under matching declared configurations. Solid arrows carry numerical data or
motion; dashed arrows carry configuration or evidence. Dispatch and checking
are separate prototypes with timing reported separately.
