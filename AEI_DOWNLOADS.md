The manuscript now follows one evidence-lifecycle argument, with a support-preservation proposition and matched comparisons against conventional DAG caching and an independently implemented rule procedure. Numerical caching benefits and evidence-processing benefits are measured and attributed separately.

The new primary simulation contains 512 independently sampled scenes and 3,072 producer outputs, with four fastener layouts, missing sites, partial occlusion, variable appearance and CAD discrepancy. A separate 128-scene stress population exposes degraded coverage and mapping. All complete methods retain 41/48 supported contexts. Exact fallback recovers three contexts, and evidence-record evaluations fall from 10,752 to 6,656 (38.10%) relative to DAG exact caching.

All 640 source-renewal cases per method are actually executed. Whole renewal consumes 183.993 seconds of process CPU, compared with 80.486 for the DAG cache, 80.279 for the contract and 79.757 for the rule implementation. The numerical-stage counts are identical for the three DAG-based paths; that benefit is not attributed exclusively to the contract. Evidence-catalogue construction is measured separately.

A 64-input numerical regression study detects 86 activated hidden couplings with no vetoes among 192 clean update pairs. The same held-out image outputs also drive source/version/history traces, including producer change, restoration, invalid attachment and independent successor recovery. These traces are identified as protocol-conformance evaluations.

Two real, openly reusable aircraft-surface photographs remain in Figure 1 with original project photographs. Their exact sources and licenses, provenance, AI-assisted writing, earlier baseline attribution, funding and implementation scope are provided in the supplement's final section. Both PDFs retain actual simulation imagery and use no generative scene artwork.

Independent re-execution matched all 3,200 main/stress observations and archived example pixels. A separate direct pipeline, bypassing the cache, matched 192 runs from 32 inputs to maximum quantity error 3.56e-15. The independently rebuilt PDFs have identical text and all seven new figure rasters match. All 176 preceding experimental/source files remain byte-identical. Numerical, provenance, reference and layout checks passed.
