\subsection{Units and task definitions}
The full acquisition event fails on GSD above 0.020 m/pixel, blur above 0.50 pixel or correct target coverage below 0.50. Full association fails on coverage below 0.50, reprojection above 30 pixels, displacement above 0.10 m or absent positive version at capture. Full reporting directly combines these failures with delay above 2 s or age above 1.8 s. It is a directly calibrated joint event, not a conjunction of independent marginal claims.

Partial requirements use GSD 0.040, blur 0.75, coverage 0.20, reprojection 50, displacement 0.15, delay 3 and age 2.8 in their stated units. Partial-relaxed removes GSD and reprojection limits, with delay 4 and age 3.8. Stricter requirements use 0.010, 0.35, 0.70, 15, 0.06, 1 and 0.8. Coarser-GSD, more-blur and late-report variants change only their named full threshold. Single acquisition retains only GSD 0.040 and blur 0.75; single association retains displacement 0.15 and readiness; single reporting retains delay 3 and age 2.8. Missing/nonfinite required quantities fail. Exact compiled-event duplicates are retained as request labels but deduplicated in the preparation benchmark.

Requirement compilation supports meter/millimeter/centimeter and second/millisecond conversions, pixel units and ratios; incompatible dimensions, Boolean and nonfinite thresholds, unknown quantities and empty intervals are rejected. The preflight compares independently implemented containment predicates on one million generated metric vectors over 10,000 event pairs and 144 task-specific fixtures. These are software tests, not simulated source observations.

\subsection{Statistical assumptions and sensitivity}
All binary event cards use the reserved common $K=4,608$, family error 0.05 and exact beta inverse-survival calculation. The executed catalogue contains 3,072 cards, including conservatively duplicated event identities. An invariant B card pools the independent A/B blocks for its event; other cards use the matched study block. Calibration independence concerns distinct complete-source draws. Policy/service/request repetitions and event dependence do not provide additional $n$ and do not invalidate the finite-family union bound.

All 3,072 full-precision cards, 6,144 plans and their confirmation counts are provided as CSV/JSON. Main-text diagnostic prefixes and alternative confidence levels are not jointly certified across their grid and do not change F1. Successful metadata/dependency checks concern a trusted frozen program under its supported population. They are not a distribution-shift detector, physical-device attestation or conditional per-image guarantee.

\subsection{Source receipts, fault fixtures and cost boundaries}
Thirty-two excluded malformed packets change one of source identity, source seal, profile, study, policy, event, plan seal or card ID. All are refused by each corresponding complete validator; the trusted source's refusal persists after gate reconstruction. Correct trusted successors remain eligible. Zero incorrect authorization in these fixed fixtures is not a population failure-rate estimate. No manipulation of the received source ID can redirect the trusted history latch.

The A request stream performs 97,280 canonical receipt checks per semantic method, 119,808 for direct renewal and 72,704 for exact matching. B performs 112,640, 136,192 and 87,040 respectively. Different counts follow supported configurations at all four allowances, not different independent sample sizes. Refused entries retain an empty policy and missing hypothetical task metrics. Canonical receipts carry a population bound, so an individual failing source can still have a valid statement.

Camera CPU, decoder CPU, native solver components, operation-clock work, first-query wall time and observer wall time have distinct boundaries. Windows thread CPU is quantized at 15.625 ms. The frozen raster/decoder clock is 62.5 ms in A and 125 ms in B, multiplied by the same declared load in report timing. It is not a physical camera's latency. Preparation uses equally preloaded records, identical event deduplication and confidence allocation; first-query batches and warmed dictionary lookups are separately measured. JSON context arrays are restored to the declared tuple fields before procedural re-query. All recomputed plans agree with F1. No human development time is inferred.
