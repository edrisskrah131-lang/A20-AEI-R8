## Research questions

1. Can explicitly compiled task-event relations support sound evidence reuse across acquisition, spatial association and timely reporting requirements?
2. Which requirement relaxations preserve the executor and population, and which producer, timing or distribution changes require recertification?
3. How do semantic reuse and cached direct recertification compare in supported requests, actual record evaluations, preparation cost and repeated-query time?
4. Can an independently implemented, fully validated procedural version achieve the same numerical and authorization outcomes? Equal or better procedural results will be retained.
