
\section{Related work}
\subsection{Engineering knowledge and executable contracts}
Process-knowledge representations and decision knowledge graphs organize heterogeneous engineering evidence for consequential choices \cite{wang2021process,hao2021knowledge}. Manufacturing decision systems extend this connection to structured reasoning \cite{su2025manufacturing}. Our representation is a small executable contract for an estimation service: its entities are source scans, posterior versions, operating profiles, computational actions, observation requests and event cards. Relations specify shared source evidence, admissible computation and the conditions for attaching a statistical statement.

Contracts for System Design provides a general mathematical treatment of assumption--guarantee reasoning and illustrates timing and scheduling contracts \cite{benveniste2018contracts}. Simplex architectures use runtime checks to govern switching between controllers \cite{mehmood2022simplex}. Our localization interface couples delayed source-version replacement to frozen statistical events whose policy, distribution and installation semantics match the request. The bounded-iteration fallback is an empirically evaluated operating mode.

\subsection{Registration, observability and delayed estimates}
ICP, generalized ICP and robust LiDAR odometry supply the geometric estimation foundations \cite{besl1992icp,segal2009gicp,pomerleau2015,kiss2023}. MAD-ICP studies robust and informed ICP odometry \cite{ferrari2024mad}; GLIM combines LiDAR--inertial estimation and mapping \cite{glim2024}. Geometry-dependent observability and degeneracy-aware updates remain central to difficult scenes \cite{xicp2024,drpm2024}. Local curvature is useful evidence but is not automatically a calibrated error distribution \cite{censi2007,bonnabel2016}.

Out-of-sequence estimation addresses measurements that arrive after their source time \cite{barshalom2002}. Our deliberately serial service revises correlated solutions of one scan by replacement and replay. It avoids the separate problem of interleaved independent measurements, which requires a rewind-and-reprocess buffer. Shared-kernel controls isolate the scheduling and contract contribution. The experiments do not establish superiority over complete KISS-ICP, MAD-ICP or GLIM systems.

\subsection{The value and reliability of computation}
Rational metareasoning and anytime algorithms view computation as a decision with an expected benefit and cost \cite{russell1991meta,zilberstein1996anytime,hay2012,callaway2018}. Estimator latency, perception scheduling and correlated fast--slow errors connect these ideas to robotic tasks \cite{pant2021,aldana2023,ghosh2021}. Changing map-registration cadence provides another computation control \cite{papais2026}. We keep opportunity cadence common and compare multi-action forecasts with shallow, deep, residual and randomized controls.

Learn then Test calibrates predictive configurations under finite-sample risk constraints, including simultaneous requirements, without refitting the predictor \cite{angelopoulos2025ltt}. We use exact binomial inversion \cite{clopper1934} and a finite-family union bound to form distribution-specific event cards. The contribution here is the localization contract connecting event identity, immutable-source versions and installation semantics to catalogue policy substitution. A metadata match checks consistency with a declared distribution assumption; it does not detect every unknown physical distribution shift.
