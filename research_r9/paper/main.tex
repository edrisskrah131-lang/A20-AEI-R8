\section*{Evidence and applicability boundary}
The new studies contain 12,800 distinct synthetic inputs: 8,192 calibration, 4,096 confirmation and 512 unsupported stress. Forty-eight pilot seeds, repeated development fixtures and five source replays add no independent evidence sample size. The 409,600 policy/service outcomes and 6,291,456 confirmation request rows are paired repetitions. Historical R8 inputs and 36 routes/432 executions remain a separate evidence block. Certificates concern unconditional frozen-policy/population events under iid sampling, matched distributions and trusted implemented event maps. They do not provide per-image conditional risk, unknown-drift detection, physical safety or embedded worst-case timing. The raster/decoder clock describes a virtual producer; receipt-observer and numerical timing boundaries are reported separately. No new real-robot experiment or route-level inspection certificate is asserted.
