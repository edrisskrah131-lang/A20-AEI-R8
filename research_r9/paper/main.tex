\section*{Evidence and applicability boundary}
The localization study contains 13,824 distinct synthetic episode inputs: 8,192 calibration, 4,096 confirmation and 1,536 unsupported stress inputs. Forty-eight additional pilot inputs are excluded from inference. Repeated policy, service and task executions add no independent sample size. The 36 complete-route inputs yield 432 executions and carry no episode-to-mission certificate. The operation clock and contention factors are declared simulation services; native CPU ledgers have explicitly different controlled and whole-online-route boundaries. Cards concern unconditional frozen-policy/population events, assume independent source draws and matched deployment distributions, and do not provide scan-conditional risk, OOD detection, physical safety or embedded worst-case timing. The attachment amendment preserves numerical/clock/event traces and withholds statements after detected invalid attempts. Original photographs and supplied simulation images are qualitative context. The separately generated inspection-task studies add 12,800 inputs (8,192 calibration, 4,096 confirmation and 512 unsupported stress), with a distinct reserved family of 4,608. Their 409,600 policy/service outcomes and 6,291,456 repeated requests add no sample size. Neither study family is pooled with the localization evidence to claim stronger confidence. No new physical robot localization experiment is reported.

\section*{Data and code availability}
The reproducibility release contains the frozen prospective protocol, all synthetic input seeds and raw arrays, native solver/predictors, policy and service specifications, calibration/confirmation/stress outcomes, complete route inputs and executions, event cards, attachment-amendment history, execution and asset hashes, analysis and vector-figure scripts, and editable publisher source. Original A19/A20 releases, four original platform photographs, five supplied simulation views and the earlier 2,680-input/81-execution study remain preserved separately. No new physical localization dataset is asserted.
