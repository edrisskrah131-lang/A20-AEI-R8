An engineering service must decide whether existing statistical evidence remains applicable when inspection requirements or producer configurations change. We extend a preserved anytime localization producer with a typed interface for image acquisition, capture-time spatial association and report delivery. Requirements compile to explicit failure events; interval containment gives a pointwise evidence-transfer witness, and event-specific dependencies delimit permissible producer changes. Exact binomial calibration and a finite simultaneous family support the stated population bounds. Two prospective synthetic studies execute 12,800 independent inputs and 409,600 paired policy/service outcomes, with actual image rasters and a predeclared wider-ROI intervention. A complete procedural semantic baseline receives the same knowledge and matches the contract's decisions; a cached direct-renewal baseline remains stronger in configured support. At risk allowance 0.15, semantic reuse supports 24/192 and 26/192 requests, versus 30/192 and 32/192 for direct renewal. Complete reporting remains uncertified. Deduplicated cold event preparation evaluates 2,490,368 records for anchor reuse versus 5,373,952 for direct renewal; all methods receive request caches. The contribution is an executable, auditable account of when engineering evidence can be reused, renewed or refused, with explicit negative results and simulation boundaries. It is not a representation-exclusive accuracy gain, general drift detector or physical safety certificate.
