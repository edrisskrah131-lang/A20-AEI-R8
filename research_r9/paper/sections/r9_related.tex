\section{Related work and scope of contribution}\label{sec:r9-related}
Engineering change analysis already formalizes relationships among requirements, behavior and structure. Wang et al. use Semantic Web representations and reasoning rules to propagate changes in a SysML system model \cite{wang2018change}. Petnga and Austin incorporate temporal, spatial and dimensional semantics in knowledge-based cyber-physical decision support \cite{petnga2016}. Ma and Hartmann explicitly connect inspection environments and defects to robot hardware requirements \cite{ma2023inspection}. These works motivate explicit domain quantities and dependencies. Our endpoint is narrower: determining the applicability of a finite-sample task-event statement after a requirement or producer change. We do not claim a new general ontology, a human productivity experiment or semantic interoperability with external CAD standards.

Contracts for system design provide established refinement and composition principles \cite{benveniste2018contracts}. Here, interval containment supplies a concrete refinement witness, while calibrated failure bounds are evidence attached to the frozen producer/population event. The proof does not derive stochastic reliability from a software type or component fingerprint. It requires both a valid statistical card and the stated event-transfer assumptions. Dependency equality concerns the specified program's data flow; fingerprints are not remote attestation of a physical device.

Distribution-free risk calibration can operate without refitting a predictive model. Learn then Test connects calibration to multiple testing \cite{angelopoulos2025ltt}; conformal risk control treats broader losses \cite{angelopoulos2024crc}. Recent preprint work also studies non-monotone, multidimensional losses \cite{angelopoulos2026nonmonotone}. We retain exact binomial bounds for binary engineering events and a finite simultaneous family. Our containment argument reuses an already valid event bound; it does not replace those calibration methods or establish a new generic risk-control theorem. In particular, an after-observation choice among different calibrated anchors does not inherit a uniform risk bound merely by choosing the smallest upper bound.

Point-cloud registration and degeneracy-aware localization remain important numerical foundations \cite{besl1992icp,chen1992,xicp2024,drpm2024}. The retained solver and predictors are held fixed in the new studies. Numerical publication, source replacement and motion replay are separate from evidence authorization. This separation permits a fair test of task knowledge without attributing an unchanged estimator's performance to the representation layer. The strongest procedural comparator implements all current semantic and dependency rules; a result shared by both implementations is attributed to those rules rather than to their storage format.
